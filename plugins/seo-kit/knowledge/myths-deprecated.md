# Myths and outdated advice

> Updated 2026-10-11 (sources last checked 2026-10-03). Primary source: Google. Secondary: SEJ (October 2025 – October 2026).
> Use: a filter against wrong advice. Before recommending anything, check that it is not in this table. If an item is present on the site, report it as "useless but harmless" unless the Reality column says it is harmful.
> `[G](url)` = Google (documentation, blog, Office Hours) · `[SEJ YYYY-MM]` = reported by Search Engine Journal (for retired features: a news item about a Google announcement) · *(re-verify)* = check on the Google changelog (`developers.google.com/search/updates`) before citing it as final · ⚠ = deprecated, unsupported by Google, or otherwise not to be recommended (meaning as in `conventions.md`).
> Topic tags: AI · CONT (content/on-page) · TECH (crawling/indexing) · SD (structured data/SERP) · LINK · GSC (measurement) · LOC (international/local).

## Notes
- *(re-verify)* The FAQ/HowTo retirement dates and the removal of the "JavaScript disabled" test come from SEJ articles reporting the Google changelog; the notes taken from the Google pages read do not contain the text of the announcement (the current gallery, however, lists neither FAQ nor HowTo). The AMP cache/SXG change is also reported by SEJ only.
- Cloudflare: since 15 September 2026 the AI crawler settings ("AI training", Bot Preference Sync) can block multi-purpose crawlers; SEJ reports conflicting accounts of the effect on Googlebot [SEJ 2026-08 vs 2026-10]. Not a myth but a risk to check at every audit: see check AI-09 in `ai-search.md`.
