About Northwind Physio

Northwind Physio opened in 2019 in central Oslo. We are three physiotherapists with backgrounds in sports medicine and orthopaedic rehabilitation.

We keep appointments long enough to examine properly and explain what we find. Exercise plans are written for the equipment you actually have at home.
