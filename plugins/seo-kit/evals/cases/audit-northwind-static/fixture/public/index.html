Physiotherapy in central Oslo

Northwind Physio is a physiotherapy clinic near Oslo Central Station. Our three physiotherapists treat back and neck pain, running and sports injuries, and help people recover after orthopaedic surgery.

Every first visit starts with a 45-minute assessment: we ask how the problem started, test movement and strength, and agree on a plan you can follow at home between sessions.

What we treat

  • Lower back and neck pain
  • Running injuries: knee, shin and Achilles problems
  • Rehabilitation after knee and shoulder surgery
